## About me

Dao Cong Tinh is currently a lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at Can Tho University (CTU), Vietnam, and a Ph.D. student in the EECS International Graduate Program at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan. His research interests span Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), Large Language Models (LLMs), and Biomedical \& Healthcare Informatics, with a particular focus on developing intelligent systems that tackle real-world challenges in healthcare and AI-driven applications.
