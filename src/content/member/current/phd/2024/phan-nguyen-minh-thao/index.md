## About Me
Phan Nguyen Minh Thao is currently a lecturer in Faculty of Information Systems at College of Information and Communication Technology at Can Tho University (CTU), Vietnam. She received a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems from CTU, Vietnam in 2021, and a Master's degree in Electrical Engineering & Computer Science (EECS) from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan in 2024, where she was a Taiwan Scholarship recipient. She is currently a PhD student in EECS at NYCU with an NSTC scholarship. She is a dedicated and curious researcher specializing in Information Systems and Computer Science.
